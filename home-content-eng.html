About Legal Studies

The Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang was established in 2001 with the objective of providing high-quality legal education relevant to contemporary developments. From the outset, the faculty has been dedicated to cultivating professional, ethical, and integrity-driven legal graduates through a curriculum that integrates classical legal theory with contemporary practice.

The Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang continuously offers opportunities to all segments of society to attain higher education at an affordable cost while upholding superior quality, particularly in human resource development through advancing doctoral (Ph.D./S3) qualifications among faculty members. In addition, our teaching staff comprises academics, legal practitioners, and impactful researchers.

The Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang continues to advance learning methodologies through regular legal curriculum updates responsive to both national and international legal dynamics. This includes interdisciplinary studies combining legal knowledge with economics, technology, and social sciences, as well as maintaining a balanced blend of theoretical knowledge and hands-on practice through moot court simulations, internships, and clinical legal education.

The Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang consistently improves its facilities and infrastructure, including comfortable classrooms, a comprehensive law library, and moot court laboratories to deepen students' mastery of procedural law. Furthermore, strategic partnerships are maintained with government bodies such as the Ministry of Law and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia, the Tangerang District Court, and other leading institutions. This aligns with Universitas Pamulang's Vision and Mission of sharing for the nation and striving to be among the Top 40 National Universities guided by the values of humanism and religiosity.
