Study Program Vision

“To produce excellent law graduates with specialized expertise in industrial relations who create a meaningful impact on society, grounded in religiosity and humanism.”

Study Program Mission

  1. To organize high-quality legal education and teaching that is responsive to national legal developments and the needs of the broader community.
  2. To conduct innovative and competitive research in legal science and industrial relations oriented toward public benefit.
  3. To carry out community service through legal advocacy, consultation, and education grounded in justice.
  4. To develop strategic partnerships with law enforcement institutions and the professional sector, guided by the values of religiosity and humanism.

Study Program Objectives

  1. To produce law graduates who are principled, professional, and proficient in both theoretical and practical legal skills.
  2. To generate high-quality legal research that provides tangible contributions to national legal reform and labor law.
  3. To actively contribute to law enforcement and the protection of legal rights for the general public.
  4. To establish transparent, accountable, and excellent governance in academic services for the study program.

Study Program Targets

  1. Enhancing graduate competency in procedural legal skills, moot court practice, and industrial relations law.
  2. Increasing the percentage of graduates directly employed in judicial institutions, government agencies, and prominent law firms.
  3. Expanding strategic partnerships for student internships and clinical legal education programs.
  4. Elevating faculty quality through doctoral-educated (Ph.D.) academic staff and active legal practitioners.
