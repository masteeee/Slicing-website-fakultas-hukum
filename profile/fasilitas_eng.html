Moot Court Room

The Moot Court Room of the Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang, is a practical learning facility designed to provide students with simulated trial experiences. With an atmosphere closely resembling an actual courtroom, this facility supports the applied learning of procedural law through court practice, ranging from reading the indictment, examining witnesses, presenting evidence, to reading verdicts. This room is utilized for practicums, moot court activities, litigation training, and various other academic pursuits. Its presence reflects the commitment of the Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang, to producing graduates who are professional, possess integrity, and demonstrate legal competence ready to be applied in the professional world.

Classrooms

The Classrooms of the Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang, are designed to be conducive and comfortable to support daily teaching and learning activities. Equipped with modern supporting facilities such as air conditioning (AC), projectors, and ergonomic seating arrangements, these classrooms create an interactive academic environment between lecturers and students in exploring various legal disciplines.

Auditorium

The Auditorium of the Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang, is a versatile large-capacity facility used to host various major events, such as national seminars, public lectures, graduation ceremonies (judicium), law symposiums, and other grand student events, fully supported by professional sound and lighting systems.

Library

The Library provides thousands of legal literature references, ranging from statutory books, scientific journals, reference textbooks on civil law, criminal law, constitutional law, to scientific works of the academic community. This room is designed to be quiet and comfortable to support research activities and independent study for students.

Teleconference Room

The Teleconference Room is equipped with state-of-the-art audiovisual communication technology to facilitate remote online meetings, international guest lectures, inter-university research collaborations, and professional virtual panel hearings.

Campus Mosque

A clean, spacious, and comfortable campus mosque is provided within the university environment to ensure all students, lecturers, and staff can perform their prayers with tranquility, serving as the central hub for campus religious and spiritual activities.

Campus Garden

The lush, green, and neatly arranged campus garden area is designed as an open space for students to have relaxed discussions, work on group assignments, or simply unwind amidst a busy lecture schedule.
