FAKULTAS HUKUM

The Law Study Program is an academic program that explores various legal systems related to social life. Students in the Law Study Program also study legislation, including fundamental areas of law such as constitutional law, civil law, commercial law, constitutional and administrative law, criminal law, criminal procedure law, and international law, which covers a broad range of legal issues.

Graduates of the program are awarded the Bachelor of Law degree (Sarjana Hukum or S.H.), with areas of concentration including Civil Law, Criminal Law, Constitutional Law, and International Law.

The Law Study Program also offers an advantage through a curriculum developed with reference to the body of knowledge, focusing on strengthening research competencies, legal scholarship, and practical application skills in the field of law.

The program develops legal knowledge, research, and community service as part of the Three Pillars of Higher Education (Tridharma Perguruan Tinggi) in an integrated manner. These activities are carried out through lectures that optimize the use of facilities and infrastructure, as well as students' individual potential throughout the learning process.

Students who complete this program are expected to have a solid understanding of Indonesian law and its legal system, possess the scientific knowledge and skills needed to advance legal studies, demonstrate sensitivity to issues of justice and society, identify and analyze legal problems, and apply laws and regulations to resolve legal issues.

Law graduates are expected to demonstrate integrity and professional ethics grounded in humanistic and religious values. These principles are reflected in professional codes of ethics and applicable legal practice regulations, with the aim of contributing to a just and democratic constitutional system.
