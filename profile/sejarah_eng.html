The History of
Faculty of Law

Universitas Pamulang was founded by the Prima Jaya Foundation in 2000, and subsequently its management was transferred to the Sasmita Jaya Foundation in 2004 under the leadership of Dr. (H.C) Drs. H. Darsono.

Under the auspices of the Sasmita Jaya Foundation and over the passage of time, Universitas Pamulang has continued to develop with remarkable progress. Starting from just one student per class and having only a single relatively small campus, today the student body has reached nearly 100,000 students across 4 campus locations—namely the main campus (Campus 1) on Jalan Surya Kencana, Campus 2 in Viktor, Campus 3 in Witana Harja, and Campus 4 (PSDKU) in Serang City.

The strides achieved by Universitas Pamulang are inseparable from the dedicated role of the Chairman of the Sasmita Jaya Foundation. The guiding purpose of the Sasmita Jaya Foundation has always been to provide affordable education accessible to all levels of society while upholding superior educational quality.

The Undergraduate Law Study Program (S-1) was established through the Decree of the Minister of National Education of the Republic of Indonesia No. 136/D/O/2001 regarding the Granting of Operational Permits for Study Programs and the Establishment of Universitas Pamulang in Tangerang organized by the Prima Jaya Foundation in Tangerang. At that time, 10 study programs were founded, with Law being one of them.
