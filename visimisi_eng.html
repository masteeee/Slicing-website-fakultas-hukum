Study Program Vision

"To produce graduates who excel in the field of law and industrial relations, making a meaningful impact on society, guided by the values of religiosity and humanism."

Study Program Mission

  1. To provide high-quality legal education and teaching that is responsive to the development of national law and the needs of the wider community.
  2. To conduct innovative and competitive research in the fields of law and industrial relations, oriented toward public benefit.
  3. To carry out community service through legal advocacy, consultation, and education grounded in justice.
  4. To develop strategic partnerships with law enforcement institutions and the professional world, founded on the values of religiosity and humanism.

Study Program Goals

  1. To produce law graduates with integrity, professionalism, and mastery of legal skills both theoretically and practically.
  2. To produce high-quality legal research that contributes meaningfully to the reform of national law and labor law.
  3. To actively contribute to law enforcement and the protection of the legal rights of the wider community.
  4. To establish transparent, accountable, and excellent study program governance in academic services.

Study Program Objectives

  1. Enhancing graduate competency in procedural law skills, moot court, and industrial relations law.
  2. Increasing the percentage of graduates directly absorbed by judicial institutions, government agencies, and reputable law firms.
  3. Expanding strategic internship and clinical legal education (law clinic) partnerships for students.
  4. Improving the quality of teaching staff holding Doctoral (S-3) degrees and active legal practitioners.
