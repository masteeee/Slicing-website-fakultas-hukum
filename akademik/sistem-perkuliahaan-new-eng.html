Schedule Options Perkuliahan Flexible

To provide maximum flexibility for regular students as well as working professionals, the Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang provides 4 lecture schedule options:

Class A (Morning) Regular Morning
Days: Monday – Friday (Morning)

Designed for full-time students focused on on-campus face-to-face interaction during morning hours.

Class B (Evening) Regular Evening/Night
Days: Monday – Friday (Evening)

The ideal choice for students who work during the day and pursue quality legal studies in the late afternoon and evening.

Class CK Special Regular
Days: Every Thursday

An intensive lecture schedule on Thursdays designed efficiently and structurally for students with specific work dynamics.

Class CS Saturday Regular
Days: Every Saturday (Weekend)

A full weekend schedule that is highly popular among professionals, practitioners, and employees aiming to upgrade their bachelor of laws qualification.

Holistic Learning Models & Methods

The Undergraduate Law Study Program implements a theory- and practice-based learning model to ensure students gain a profound understanding of legal concepts and can apply them in real-world contexts through 6 primary methods:

Face-to-Face (Offline & Hybrid)
A combination of direct interaction in air-conditioned classrooms and flexible interactive hybrid learning.
Interactive Discussion & Case Studies
Dissecting recent court rulings to sharpen critical thinking, legal reasoning, and juridical arguments.
Moot Court Simulation (Mock Trial)
Hands-on practice in civil and criminal court proceedings at the Law Laboratory to hone litigation skills.
Visits to Legal Institutions
Periodic study visits to the Constitutional Court (MK), Ministry of Law and Human Rights (Kemenkumham), Supreme Court, and District Courts.
Internships & Independent MBKM
The Merdeka Belajar Kampus Merdeka program at leading law firms, corporations, notary offices, and government agencies.
Digital Technology & AI Legal Analysis
LMS integration on the student portal, utilization of AI for regulatory and case analysis, and interactive gamification.

Credit (SKS) Regulations & Thesis Completion Pathways

Students of the Undergraduate Law Study Program (S-1) complete a cumulative total study load of 146 Credits dalam 8 semester (4 tahun):

Course Distribution & Study Load
  • 57 Courses encompassing Basic Legal Theory, Judicial Practice Law, and Elective Specialization Law.
  • Pengambilan Credit Load per semester is adjusted according to the student's Grade Point Average (GPA) evaluation.
  • Effective Study Duration: 8 Semesters (4 Years) with acceleration options for high-achieving students.
Undergraduate Thesis Proposal Requirements & Schedule
Thesis proposals may be submitted starting from Semester 7 by fulfilling the following requirements:
✓ Has completed a minimum of 120 Credits
✓ Passed course Legal Research Methods (Semester 5)
✓ Passed course Thesis Writing Methods (Semester 6)
Thesis Completion Timeframe: 1 – 2 Semesters
