Official Undergraduate Curriculum (LL.B. / S.H.)

Course Structure & Credit Distribution

Official curriculum of the Bachelor of Law Program (S-1), Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang with a cumulative workload of 148 Credits (SKS), featuring 4 major concentrations: Civil Law, Criminal Law, Constitutional Law, and International Law.

ⓘ Academic Guidelines & Specialization Track Policy
  • The curriculum framework strictly adheres to the Latest Academic Handbook of Bachelor of Law Program, Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang.
  • Selection of Specialization Concentration is conducted upon entering Semester 5 (Civil Law, Criminal Law, Constitutional Law, or International Law).
  • Completion of the Bachelor of Law degree requires 148 SKS Credits (including Moot Court / Litigation Practicum and 6 SKS Undergraduate Thesis).
