ⓘ About the Outcome-Based Education (OBE) Curriculum Model
  • The Outcome-Based Education (OBE) framework applies a backward design principle: starting with industry-demanded Graduate Profiles, deriving Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs), and structuring supporting coursework accordingly.
  • Every course is designed with direct constructive alignment to fulfill target ILO competencies for students at the Faculty of Law, Universitas Pamulang.
  • Students are encouraged to consult with their Academic Advisors (DPA) to choose specialized tracks aligned with their targeted legal careers.
