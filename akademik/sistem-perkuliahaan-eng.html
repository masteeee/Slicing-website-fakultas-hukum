Flexible Lecture Schedule Options

To provide maximum flexibility for both regular students and working professionals, the Faculty of Law offers four lecture schedule options:

Class A (Morning) Regular Morning
⏰ Days: Monday – Friday (Morning)

Designed for full‑time students who prefer face‑to‑face interaction in the morning.

Class B (Evening) Regular Evening
⏰ Days: Monday – Friday (Evening)

Ideal for students who work during the day and continue their law studies in the late afternoon/evening.

Class CK Special Regular
⏰ Day: Every Thursday

Intensive Thursday schedule designed for students with specific professional dynamics.

Class CS Regular Saturday
⏰ Day: Every Saturday (Weekend)

Popular weekend schedule for professionals, practitioners, and employees who wish to earn a law degree.

Holistic Learning Model & Methods

The Law program implements a theory‑practice based learning model to ensure deep understanding of legal concepts and their real‑world application through six main methods:

🏛️
Face‑to‑Face (Offline & Hybrid)
Combination of in‑person classes with AC and flexible hybrid learning.
💡
Interactive Discussions & Case Studies
Analyze recent court decisions to sharpen critical reasoning, legal logic, and argumentation.
⚖️
Mock Court Simulation (Moot Court)
Hands‑on civil and criminal trial procedures in the Law Laboratory to develop litigation skills.
🏢
Visits to Legal Institutions
Regular study visits to the Constitutional Court, Ministry of Law & Human Rights, Supreme Court, and district courts.
🚀
Internships & Independent MBKM
Freedom to learn in top law firms, corporations, notary offices, or government agencies.
🤖
Digital Technology & AI Legal Analysis
Integration of LMS portal, AI‑assisted legal regulation analysis, and interactive gamification.

Credit Requirements & Thesis Proposal Roadmap

Law students must complete a total of 146 credits over 8 semesters (4 years):

📚 Course Distribution & Credit Load
  • 57 Courses covering Basic Legal Theory, Court Practice, and Specialized Law.
  • Credit Allocation per semester adjusted according to GPA.
  • Effective Study Period: 8 Semesters (4 Years) with acceleration options for high‑achieving students.
🎓 Requirements & Timeline for Thesis Proposal Submission
Thesis proposals can be submitted starting Semester 7 upon meeting the following criteria:
✓ Completed at least 120 credits
✓ Passed Legal Research Methods (Semester 5)
✓ Passed Thesis Writing Methods (Semester 6)
⏱️ Thesis Completion Time: 1 – 2 Semesters
