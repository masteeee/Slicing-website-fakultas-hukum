Curriculum Map Constructive Alignment Diagram
Three integrated, mutually reinforcing curriculum pillars:
1
Profil Lulusan
Professional roles of graduates in the workplace
  • Lawyer / Advocate & Legal Practitioner
  • Regulatory & Contract Drafter (Legal Drafter)
  • Business Legal Consultant / In-House Counsel
  • Public Law Enforcement Apparatus (Judge, Prosecutor, Investigator)
  • Legal Academic / Researcher & Policy Analyst
2
Learning Outcomes (CPL)
Competency standards and graduate capabilities
  • Attitude & Ethics: Moral integrity and humanistic values
  • Juridical Knowledge: Substantive and procedural legal theory
  • Specialized Skills: Courtroom practice and litigation
  • Legal Drafting: Drafting contracts and regulations
  • Critical Analysis: Legal reasoning and dispute resolution
3
Mata Kuliah Pendukung
Curriculum supporting CPL mastery
  • Foundational Courses: Introduction to Legal Studies, Introduction to Indonesian Law, Legal Logic
  • Substantive Courses: Criminal, Civil, Constitutional Law, Administrative Law
  • Procedural Courses: Procedural Law and Evidence
  • Practicum & Clinical: Practical Legal Skills (Litigation & Non-Litigation)
  • Final Project: Undergraduate Thesis (6 Credits)

Pengacara / Advokat & Praktisi Hukum
Graduate Profile #1 • Litigation & Non-Litigation
View Alignment • 1 → 2 → 3 ▼
1. Professional Role Description (Graduate Profile)
A profession providing legal services, advocacy in criminal, civil, state administrative, and religious court cases, as well as negotiation assistance and out-of-court dispute resolution mediation.
▼ Diwujudkan melalui Learning Outcomes (CPL)
2. Graduate Learning Outcomes (Competencies to Achieve)
CPL-A (Ethical Integrity)
Upholding the advocate's code of ethics, moral integrity in law enforcement, substantive justice, and human rights protection.
CPL-B (Procedural Law Expertise)
Mastering substantive law (Civil, Criminal, Administrative) and all stages of procedural law across court jurisdictions in Indonesia.
CPL-C (Advocacy & Litigation Skills)
Capable of drafting courtroom legal documents (power of attorney, lawsuits, replies, defense pleas, appeals/cassation briefs) and practicing before courts.
CPL-D (Alternative Dispute Resolution / Non-Litigation)
Skilled in mediation, negotiation, and conciliation techniques to resolve disputes peacefully and constructively.

Explanation of the OBE Curriculum Map Concept
  • The Outcome-Based Education (OBE) approach designs the curriculum through backward design: starting by defining the Graduate Profiles demanded by professions, deriving Graduate Learning Outcomes (CPL), and then structuring relevant Courses.
  • Every course maintains direct constructive alignment with the fulfillment of CPL and the formation of graduate profiles for Faculty of Law UNPAM students.
  • Students can consult with their Academic Advisors (DPA) to select elective specialization courses that best align with their targeted career profile.
