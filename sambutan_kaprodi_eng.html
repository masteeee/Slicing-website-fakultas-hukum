Welcome to the website of the Bachelor of Law Study Program at Universitas Pamulang.

We offer our praise and gratitude to Allah SWT for all His blessings and grace. This website has been developed as a platform for information, communication, and academic services accessible to students, prospective students, alumni, partners, and the general public. Through this website, we are committed to providing accurate, transparent, and easily accessible information about the activities of the Bachelor of Law Study Program.

The Bachelor of Law Study Program at Universitas Pamulang is committed to delivering high-quality legal education by emphasizing academic excellence, integrity, professionalism, and the values of justice. The learning process is supported by competent lecturers, a curriculum relevant to legal developments, and a range of activities in research, community service, and student affairs that foster the emergence of outstanding and competitive graduates.

We are committed to producing law graduates who are outstanding, principled, and globally competitive. This excellence is supported by highly qualified human resources, ranging from Professors, Associate Professors, and Doctors, to seasoned practitioners. It is also backed by exceptional facilities and a strong academic atmosphere through various extracurricular programs designed to reinforce the attainment of graduate profiles.

Thank you to all leaders, lecturers, students, alumni, and partners for their continued support. We invite you to explore our website to learn more about the commitment and quality of education at the S-1 Law Study Program.
